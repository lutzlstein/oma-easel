# Logo

`logo-source.png` was generated with Codex's built-in image tool from:

> logo for Easel, an omarchy background generator. A video terminal styled after the late-1970s DEC VT100: one wide, low, beige single-body terminal with the CRT screen set into a dark bezel on the left of the front face and the body sloping back, seen in three-quarter view. No keyboard, no brand names, no logos, no lettering on the terminal. On the screen: only a simple wooden painter's easel (the A-frame stand with its ledge, no canvas, nothing on it), drawn in a few clean lines. The screen background behind the easel is minimalist and colorful: a few large flat color bands or soft geometric shapes in a cheerful palette. Flat vector logo style, bold near-black outlines of even weight, a few flat colors, no text. Square image with a transparent background.

`src/assets/logo.png` is that image trimmed to its content and scaled to 512px. It carries its own contrast (beige body, dark outline), so it is shown in full color on every theme. The window shows the app's name as text beside it, so renaming the app needs no new image.

## Wordmark font

The app name beside the mark is set in [Bitcount](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bitcount) at weight 400, by Petr van Blokland, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font and its license are in `src/assets/fonts/` and compiled into the binary.
